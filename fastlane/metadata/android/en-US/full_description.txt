The companion app for HUBIplan, the free club management software. It connects to your club's
HUBIplan instance (QR code from the member area in the browser) and shows:

* Events of your club and its units, accept or decline with one tap
* Events in the device calendar with reminders, “Response missing” notice
* Messages and circulars from the board, instantly via push through a UnifiedPush app
  such as ntfy (without Google, content end-to-end encrypted) or by polling
* Send short messages (for the board and unit leaders)
* Your own profile, report changes, take a member photo
* Your club's colours and crest, layouts for phone and tablet
* Shortcut with your club's crest and name on the home screen
* Several clubs with their own HUBIplan address in one app
* German or English, following your account or the Android app language

In the app menu the app is called HUBIplan; after signing in it shows your club.
The app only talks to your club's HUBIplan instance. It contains no ads, no analytics and no
Google services (no Firebase, no Play services). It requires that your club uses HUBIplan
and has given you access to the member area.
